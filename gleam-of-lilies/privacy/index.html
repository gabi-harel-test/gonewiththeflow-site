Privacy Policy — Gleam of Lilies

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Gleam of Lilies is a puzzle game made by Gabi Harel ("Gone with the Flow", "we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play.

What the game itself keeps

  • Your progress and settings (current garden, tile positions, sound and motion settings) are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game has no account, no sign-in, no server of ours and no analytics.
  • Deleting the app deletes this saved progress.

Ads

The game is free and shows occasional full-screen ads between gardens, provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To show ads and prevent fraud, Google's software on your device may collect information such as your device type and settings, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. This collection is done by Google under Google's own terms; we do not receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • The game asks Google for non-personalized ads only: ads are chosen from the game and general context, not from a profile of you.
  • The game does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites (no App Tracking Transparency prompt), so your device's advertising identifier is not shared.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads use your data. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy choices.
  • More about how Google uses information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

Gleam of Lilies is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent: Settings → Privacy choices (where available).
  • Stop all data collection by ads: stop playing and delete the app.
  • Questions or requests: support@gonewiththeflow.app

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
